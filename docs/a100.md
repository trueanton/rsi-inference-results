# A100 baseline qualification

Qwen3.6-27B in BF16 exceeded the memory of one 40 GiB A100. A separate diagnostic qualified on two A100s at TP=2. GPT-OSS-120B served MXFP4 on two A100s at TP=2 after raising GPU memory utilization to 95% and enabling graph-aware memory profiling.

## Qualification and solver outcome

Both models passed the public correctness check on 20 prompts. Across both unchanged-engine arms, qualification completed 9,752 requests in 44 timed runs, with zero failed requests. Qwen’s Slurm job exited 1 after a postcheck error. A recovery audit verified all 18 timed records. GPT’s correctness helper accepted the saved outputs after both child processes signaled during teardown. [Qwen recovery record](../tasks/optimize_qwen3_6_27b_1xh100_open/results/postcheck-recovery.json) · [GPT correctness exits](../tasks/optimize_gpt_oss_120b_2xh100_open/results/correctness-exits.txt) · [Variation by arm](acceptance/variability.md)

Qwen had zero solver attempts. After GPT qualification, the solver profiled the unchanged engine for another 5,648 requests before exhausting its 32,768-token context window. A fresh local verifier confirmed the empty diff and awarded 0.0 for `no_patch`. The original verifier required H100 hardware. Agent speedup was unmeasured in both tasks.
